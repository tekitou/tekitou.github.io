Privacy Policy for rclone

This application, "rclone", is used solely by the owner of this website for personal encrypted backup to Google Drive.

Google User Data Access

The application accesses the owner's Google Drive account in order to list, upload, download, create, update, and delete files required for personal backup operations.

Use of Google User Data

Google Drive data is used only for the purpose of performing personal backup and restore operations requested by the owner. The application does not use Google user data for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any unrelated purpose.

Storage

Files are encrypted locally using rclone crypt before being uploaded to Google Drive. OAuth credentials and configuration data are stored locally on the owner's device.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, transferred, or shared with third parties.

Data Retention

Data remains in Google Drive until the owner deletes it. The owner may revoke the application's Google account access at any time.
